303 Circuit Model guide

A free acid bass synth, modelled part by part from the original 1982 bass line circuit: the pitch ladder, the oscillator, the four-pole ladder filter, the envelopes and the VCA, each from its schematic values. It runs in your browser, and as a VST3 / AU plug-in on macOS, Windows and Linux. The plug-in shows exactly the same screen as the web version and makes the same sound.

Around the voice: a 64-step pattern sequencer with slides and accents, eight pattern slots you can chain, a pattern generator and randomisers, a step sequencer for any dial, a wavefolder, three drive pedals, a flanger, a tempo-synced delay, four kinds of reverb and a ducker.

Web: open the app and press ▶ (or space). Plug-in: install it, then load 303 Circuit Model as an instrument in your DAW.

1. Get it

In the browser

Open 303.johnnyhawk.org and press ▶ (or the space bar). That’s it.

  • Chrome, Edge and Safari work on a computer or tablet; Firefox works too. MIDI keyboards and controllers need Chrome or Edge.
  • On a phone or tablet, turn it sideways.
  • To use it offline, install it as an app: Options › About › Install app.
  • Everything you save stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

As a plug-in (VST3 / AU)

Paste one line into a terminal, on macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) or Linux (64-bit x86). Run the same line again any time to update. Then restart your DAW or rescan plug-ins, and look for an instrument called 303 Circuit Model.

The install page has the command for each computer, step by step, plus how to remove it. Your presets are kept either way.

Why a terminal command? The Mac plug-in isn’t notarised by Apple, so a zip downloaded in a browser is blocked as “damaged”. Fetched with curl it isn’t, and nothing else on your system is changed.

2. Your first five minutes

  1. Press ▶ (or space). Slot 1 holds a classic acid line, and slots 2 to 8 each start with a generated pattern.

  2. Turn Cutoff, Reso, Env mod and Decay on the front panel while it plays. That’s most of the acid sound.

  3. Press 2 to 8 to hear the other patterns. Press G for a brand-new pattern in the slot you’re on; Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes it.

  4. Press A, S or O for new accents, slides or octaves on the same notes. Hold Shift for a gentler change.

  5. Open the Preset menu in the top bar and try a few factory sounds with ◀ ▶. Tick Sound only to keep your patterns while you browse.

  6. Found something good? Open the Preset menu, give it a name (or take the one offered, 🎲 rolls another) and press Save.

3. The screen

PartWhat it does
Top bar▶ play, seq (sequencer on/off), the Tempo dial (60 to 200 BPM), the Preset menu with ◀ ▶, the version number and Options (the gear).
303 VoiceThe front panel: the waveform switch and the dials of the synth itself, plus the Components column.
PagesBelow the voice: Sequencer, Mods, FX and Tuning, picked by the folder tabs.

Dials

  • Drag up and down to turn. Hold Shift for fine moves.
  • Click a dial and use the arrow keys (Page Up / Page Down for big steps, Home / End for the ends).
  • Double-click resets it. On the Tempo dial, double-click lets you type a tempo.
  • Long-press or right-click a dial for its own step sequencer (see section 7).

Dials only move while you hold the button, so scrolling past them never changes anything.

4. The voice

The front panel, left to right:

ControlWhat it does
WaveformSaw or square, shown by the two little wave icons.
TuningFine pitch of the whole voice.
CutoffWhere the filter sits.
ResoResonance. Turn it up for the squelch.
Key trackHigher notes open the filter. 0 is the stock behaviour; about a third tracks the keyboard exactly; full goes much further.
Env modHow far the envelope sweeps the filter on each note.
DecayHow long that sweep takes to fall.
AccentHow hard accented steps hit: louder, and a sharper, building filter sweep.
VolumeOutput level.

Components

The column at the right:

  • Filter trimThe factory cutoff calibration trimmer. It shifts the whole filter about an octave either way, shown in semitones.
  • New unitEvery part lands somewhere inside its real tolerance, like another unit off the production line. Each press is a slightly different synth.
  • Messed upEvery part bent well outside spec, circuit-bending style. Tuning parts are left alone so it stays in tune.
  • UndoPuts the part values back.

5. Making patterns: the Sequencer page

The roll

Each column is a step; the playing step is outlined.

  • Note rows (C to B): click a cell to put a note on that step. Click the lit note again to make the step a rest.
  • Octave rows (+2, +1, −1, −2, above the notes): move a step’s note up or down an octave. Click the lit one again to go back to the middle octave.
  • SLIDE and ACC rows at the bottom: slide into the next note, and accent.
  • Press and drag to paint: the first cell’s change is laid on every step you cross, and in the note rows the stroke follows the pointer, so you can draw a line.
  • The roll always shows at least 32 steps. Steps past the pattern’s end are dimmed; click one to lengthen the pattern up to there.
  • Arrow keys move around the grid once a cell has focus.

Above the roll

  • pattern 1 to 8The eight slots. Click one (or press 1 to 8) to play and edit it. Your edits save to the slot automatically.
  • ChainsRight-click or Cmd/Ctrl-click slots to play several in a row. The chain shows next to the slots; × clears it.
  • MenusRandomise ▾ (R), Tools ▾ (T) and Gate ▾ (E). See below.

Below the roll

  • Length1 to 64 steps. Steps you shorten away come back if you lengthen again.
  • densityHow many steps Generate and Refill fill with notes (10 to 100%, default 60%). Alter leans towards it too. Double-click for 60%.
  • The D-pad▲ ▼ move every note a semitone (Shift-click for an octave); ◀ ▶ shift the whole pattern a step left or right. If a note would go off the top or bottom of the range, nothing moves, so the riff keeps its shape.

Gate ▾

How long each note sounds, as a share of the step: 10% (short and plucky) to 95% (nearly legato). Default 50%.

7. Movement: a step sequencer on any dial

Every dial except Tempo can have its own step sequence, so the cutoff, the delay time or a Mods part can move in time with the pattern.

  1. Long-press or right-click a dial. Its sequencer opens.

  2. Drag across the steps to paint them up or down from the dial’s setting. The page buttons show steps 1-16, 17-32 and so on.

  3. Set length (2 to 64 steps), slew (glide to each step, off to a whole step), speed (a 16th up to 8 bars per step) and depth (how far the steps move the dial).

  4. Or press Randomise. The button next to it picks the kind: random, drunk, wobbly, ramp, s&h or sparse. Click it to step through them.

  5. On switches the sequence off without losing it. Done (or Esc) closes it.

While it’s open: ← → pick a step, ↑ ↓ move it (Shift for bigger moves), 1 to 4 pick the page, R randomises.

A sequenced dial’s ring stays at its own setting, and a small pointer outside the ring shows where the sequence has moved it. Sequences are saved with the sound and in presets.

8. The Mods page

Twelve circuit parts, laid out like the controls of the best-known mod kits for this synth. Each has a short description under it, and the value in real units. Click the value to put that part back to stock.

9. Effects: the FX page

Each unit has an Off / On button.

  • FolderWest coast timbre: a five-cell wavefolder ahead of the drive. Timbre is how hard it folds, Symmetry offsets it, Level sets its output.
  • DrivePick the pedal from its menu. Rodent: op-amp into a diode pair (Distortion, Filter, Volume). Dist One: transistor booster into diodes to ground (Dist, Tone, Level). Green 808: diodes in the op-amp feedback, warm and mid-heavy (Drive, Tone, Level).
  • DelayTempo synced, with tone on the repeats. Time (1/32 to 1/2 note), Feedback, Low cut, High cut, Return, and Ping-pong or Mono.
  • DuckerAlways on. It ducks the delay and reverb returns while the synth plays, so the echoes swell up in the gaps. Amount and Release.
  • ReverbPick the type, then its program (see below).
  • FlangerA bucket-brigade flanger, with a Flanger / Chorus switch. Manual, Depth, Rate, Res.

Reverb types

  • Digital 224A late-70s digital reverb, nine programs (P1 Small Concert Hall B, P2 Vocal Plate, P3 Large Concert Hall B, P4 Acoustic Chamber, P5 Percussion Plate A, P6 Small Concert Hall A, P7 Room A, P8 Constant Density Plate A, P9 Chorus A). Bass and Mid decay, Crossover, Treble, Depth, Diffusion, Predelay, Mode enh, Pitch, Return.
  • PlateFive sizes, from tiny to huge. Predelay, Decay, Damping, Bandwidth, Low cut, Diffusion, Mod, Return.
  • SpringOne, two or three springs, or a long tank. Dwell, Decay, Tension, Tone, Low cut, Return.
  • ConvolutionRecorded spaces (two 140 plates, a drum room, a grain silo, a concrete garage, the Musikverein in Vienna, On a Star) and two generated ones (church, 1980s gated). Predelay, Decay, Tone, Low cut, Width, Return.

Routing

The block diagram at the bottom shows the signal path. Click a block to switch that unit on or off.

  • fold → drive or drive → fold: which comes first.
  • parallel, dly → rev (echoes are reverberated) or rev → dly (the reverb tail is echoed).

10. The Tuning page

A fine offset for each of the twelve notes, in cents, the same in every octave. All at 0 is the stock synth (equal temperament).

  • Drag a fader to retune one note.
  • Just, Pythagorean and Meantone lay out a temperament from the root note picked at the bottom (c to b).
  • Drift puts every note a few cents out at random, like an old unit whose pitch circuit has drifted.
  • Reset puts every note back to 0; Undo steps back.

Tuning is part of the sound, so presets keep it.

11. Presets

Click the preset name in the top bar to open the Preset menu. ◀ ▶ next to it step through presets without opening it.

  • Factory holds 30 ready-made sounds; My presets holds yours.
  • Search finds a preset by name; ★ at the top shows only favourites. Click the ☆ on any row to star it.
  • Save stores everything: the sound, effects, tempo, all eight pattern slots, the chain and the dial sequences. A name is suggested for you; 🎲 rolls another, or type your own.
  • Sound only: loading a preset changes only the sound and effects, and keeps your patterns and tempo.
  • × on a row deletes it (click twice). Deleted factory presets can be brought back from the “restore” row at the end of the Factory folder.
  • Export all saves all your presets to one .json file; Export favourites saves the starred ones; Import file loads either back in. Use these to move presets between browsers, computers, and the web version and the plug-in.

12. MIDI

Controllers: front-panel dials follow MIDI CC from any input, on any channel. The full list is in Options › MIDI.

CCControl
1 to 9Tuning, Cutoff, Reso, Key track, Env mod, Decay, Accent, Volume, Filter trim
10 to 21The Mods page, in order: Soft attack, Slide time, Short decay, Note length, Accent sweep, Env depth, Cutoff range, Reso limit, Drive, Ladder cap, Accent wow, Noise

Notes (any channel; C3 = middle C = note 60, as in Logic and Ableton):

  • C0 to G0 (notes 24 to 31) pick patterns 1 to 8.
  • C1 to C3 (notes 36 to 60) set the root the pattern plays from: C1 plays it as written, each semitone up transposes it up one. This is a performance setting, so it isn’t saved and comes back to C1 when you reload.

Seq off: a MIDI synth. The small seq button next to Play turns the step sequencer off. Then every MIDI note plays the synth directly: mono, last note wins, A2 = 110 Hz, velocity 100 and up is an accent, and a note played while another is held slides to it. (In a browser, click or press a key once after switching seq off, so the audio can start.)

In the browser, MIDI needs Chrome or Edge.

13. In your DAW

  • TransportThe DAW runs it: play, stop and tempo follow your DAW, and the pattern locks to its bar grid. There is no Play button or Tempo dial in the plug-in.
  • AutomationEvery dial, the effect On switches, the wave switch and the pattern slot (the Pattern parameter, 1 to 8) are DAW parameters.
  • MIDI notesWork as above, with the window open or shut. With seq off, every note plays the synth and the DAW transport doesn’t start the pattern.
  • SavingThe DAW project keeps the sound, the patterns, the chain and the seq setting. Your presets, favourites and colours are shared by every copy of the plug-in, in ~/Library/Application Support/303 Circuit Model (Mac), %APPDATA%\303 Circuit Model (Windows) or ~/.config/303 Circuit Model (Linux).
  • Known limitsMIDI CC only reaches the plug-in while its window is open (automation and notes work with it closed), and dial step sequences also run only while the window is open. The heaviest settings (convolution reverb plus every effect) want a reasonably recent computer.

Not installed yet? See Install 303 Circuit Model.

14. Make it yours

Options (the gear in the top bar) has three tabs:

  • MIDIThe CC and note list.
  • ColoursGrey + colour (grey glass with one spot colour, the default) or Mono (the whole screen in one colour); six colours (orange, amber, lime, blue, red, grey); and Brightness, Saturation, Dirt and Grain sliders for the glass, with Reset.
  • AboutThe version, Install app, the licence, and notes on how each part of the circuit was modelled.

Esc closes Options, menus and the dial sequencer.
