FM/DM guide

FM/DM (frequency modulated drum machine) is a free six-voice FM drum machine. Every sound is made from scratch with 4-operator FM, a pitch envelope, click, noise, filter and crush. It runs in your browser and as a VST3 / AU plug-in and standalone app.

Web: open the app and press ▶ play (or space). Plug-in: install it, then load fm-dm as an instrument in your DAW.

In your DAW

  • TransportThe plug-in has no play or rec buttons. Your DAW’s transport starts and stops it, and it follows the DAW tempo. Record its output on a track as usual. In the standalone app, press space to play and stop.
  • MIDI notesMIDI notes play the drums. General MIDI drum notes work (36 kick, 38 snare, 42 closed hat, 46 open hat, 49 cymbal, toms for drum 2 and drum 3). From MIDI note 60 (C3 in most DAWs) upwards, each run of six notes plays the six drums in order.
  • PresetsPresets move freely between the web version and the plug-in.

Not installed yet? See Install FM/DM.

The screen

The header holds randomise ▾, options, ? (help) and, on the web, ▶ play and ● rec. Along the left are 24 perf knobs and the mutes. The pages on the right are:

PageWhat it does
beatPattern generator: tempo, genre (techno, house, electro, breaks, dnb, hiphop, garage, minimal), beat, energy, fills and length. feel · rush sets swing, accent and drum rolls near the end of every n bars. density per drum, then the step grid (click toggles, shift-click accents, right-click a hat step for open).
voicePick a drum (kick, drum 2, drum 3, snare, cymbal, hihat). Click to audition, shift-click the hat for open. Tabs: tone/fm (algorithm, tune, FM, operators A to D), shape (pitch env, click, amp, crush), filter (filter, noise, bursts), mod (LFO, velocity, wobble, sends).
fxecho (sync, ping-pong), reverb with an algorithmic or impulse response mode (30 factory IRs, including recorded spaces from Voxengo, or load your own ir), and the distortion and ring mod send buses.
xmodA 6×6 patchbay where each drum’s base operator modulates the others. Randomise or clear it.
mixA strip per drum: sends, pan, level fader with meter, mute and solo.
mstrKick sidechain duck, master distortion, compressor with gain-reduction meter, output.
prstPresets: built-ins plus your own (saved in the browser under two-word names). Favourite, search, save new or save over, export and import. Loading while playing waits for the next bar.

Knobs and motion

  • Drag up and down (shift for fine), scroll, or click a knob and use the arrow keys. Double-click resets.
  • Right-click any knob or fader to assign it to a perf knob, give it a step sequencer, MIDI-learn it, or reset it.
  • Step sequencer: click a knob, then paint steps in the strip under perf. Length 2 to 64, slew, speed (a 16th up to 8 bars a step), depth, shapes, and slide or env mode. Sequencers follow the swing.

Randomise

Open randomise ▾: everything, kit, alter, pattern, xmod, random fx, alter fx and motion (puts step sequences on a few random knobs), plus undo.

  • Drag a button up or down to set its depth. Hold it for its ranges and chances.
  • go mental runs every randomiser at full depth with wild ranges.
  • Right-click (or long-press on touch) a drum on the voice page to randomise just that drum.
  • In options: randomise on next bar, wild ranges (not drum-safe), and auto-level drums after randomise. Options also sets the screen colour.

Mutes act on notes, so tails ring on.

MIDI

CC 1 to 24 drive the perf knobs on any channel. CC 25 to 28 set the selected sequencer’s length, slew, speed and depth. Right-click a knob and pick midi learn to map your own CC.

Recording (web)

● rec records a WAV, either free-running or an exact loop of 1 to 128 bars starting on the next bar.
