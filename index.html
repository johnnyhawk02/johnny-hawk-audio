Elixir Desktop

A six-voice drum machine in the browser. Its engine is a sample-exact JavaScript port of the LXR-02 drum firmware.

Play it Source
  • Voices6: three drum/tom, snare/clap, cymbal, hi-hat
  • Kits28 bundled, plus any LXR .SND file
  • FX busDistortion, ring mod, compressor, delay
  • Routing6 × 6 crossfeed patchbay
  • Accuracy176 million samples checked against the firmware, all identical

Mono synth · VST3 prototype

Elixir Synth

One voice of the Elixir engine played as a synth, with a note sequencer and an LCD screen. It is laid out as the prototype of a VST3 plugin, and it installs as an app that works offline.

Play it Source
  • VoiceLXR drum 1 (DR1), every parameter on four tabs
  • Sequencer4 pages of 16 steps with gate, accent and slide
  • Random10 algorithms, from dice and acid to euclid and arp
  • FXEcho, reverb, ring mod, then distortion and compressor on the mix
  • MIDICC 1 to 8 turn the eight performance dials
