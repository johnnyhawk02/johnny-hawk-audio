Elixir Desktop

A six-voice drum machine built on the LXR-02 engine, with step sequencers on every control. Play it in the browser, or load it in your music software as a VST3, Audio Unit, CLAP or LV2 plugin. Free and open source.

  • Kits28 bundled, plus any LXR .SND file
  • FX busDistortion, ring mod, compressor, delay
  • Routing6 × 6 crossfeed patchbay
  • Accuracy176 million samples checked against the firmware, all identical
  • FormatsVST3, Audio Unit, CLAP, LV2 and a standalone app

Mono synth · VST3 prototype

Elixir Synth

One voice of the Elixir engine played as a synth, with a note sequencer and an LCD screen. It is laid out as the prototype of a VST3 plugin, and it installs as an app that works offline.

Play it Source
  • VoiceLXR drum 1 (DR1), every parameter on four tabs
  • Sequencer4 pages of 16 steps with gate, accent and slide
  • Random10 algorithms, from dice and acid to euclid and arp
  • FXEcho, reverb, ring mod, then distortion and compressor on the mix
  • MIDICC 1 to 8 turn the eight performance dials

Bass synth · circuit model

TB-303 Circuit Model

A bass synth in your browser that recreates the Roland TB-303 part by part. Instead of imitating the sound, it models the real circuit, including the resistors, capacitors and the famous filter, so every knob behaves like the original hardware. Generate fresh acid lines from classic pattern material, build a “new unit” within factory tolerances or a messed-up one, and run it through a RAT distortion and Lexicon 224-style reverb. It works offline and on your phone.

Watch on YouTube

  • Pitch6-bit R-2R ladder, 1/12 V per step
  • Filter4-pole transistor/diode ladder, 2× oversampled
  • EffectsRAT distortion and a Lexicon 224 reverb, both modelled from their parts
  • PatternsFresh acid lines generated from classic pattern material
  • UnitsA new unit within factory tolerances, or a messed-up one
